Harbor Arts Hall accessibility profile. Step-free continuity: the north entrance, lift lobby, level two corridor, and Studio 4 are connected without stairs. Width and clearance: every doorway on this route is at least 920 millimetres. Gradient and surface: the entrance ramp is 1:20 with a firm non-slip surface. Restroom access: an accessible restroom is available beside the level two lift. Evacuation support: the refuge point has two-way communication and trained staff assistance.
